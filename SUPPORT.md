# Support & Community Guidance

Welcome to **ContainerGuard**. This document outlines how to seek technical guidance and report findings for the project.

---

## Technical Guidance & Documentation

Before opening an issue or discussion, review the technical documentation suite:
- **[README.md](README.md):** Executive overview, eBPF internals, detection rules, and quickstart.
- **[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md):** Deep-dive architecture and component specifications.
- **[BUILD.md](BUILD.md):** Comprehensive compilation, testing, and troubleshooting guide.
- **[EXPERIMENTS.md](EXPERIMENTS.md):** Experimental evaluation protocols and benchmark methodologies.
- **[docs/](docs/):** Technical audit, stress, saturation, and acceptance reports.

---

## Where to Ask Questions

- **Bug Reports:** If you discover an error in an implemented 50% milestone component, open a [Bug Report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.yml).
- **Documentation Clarifications:** For ambiguities, broken links, or corrections, submit a [Documentation Issue](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/documentation.yml).
- **Research Collaboration & Experiments:** For methodological discussions or benchmark replications, open a [Research Discussion](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/research_experiment.yml).
